# Voices a fresh machine could be offered (research, 2026-11-21)

For roadmap F: the extension offers to download a voice from its author's own address, shows
its terms, and installs it. This is which voices' terms allow that, read from the terms
themselves on 2026-11-21. No terms page read says anything about a tool fetching the files
for a user; "may be redistributed" is taken as covering it (a fetch from the author's address
is less than redistribution).

## Qixuan's terms, the clauses that decide (github.com/yqzhishen/qixuan-diffsinger, terms_of_use/Terms_of_Use.zh-CN.md, 2.7.0)

- Songs: "基于商业或非商业用途，使用此声库的产出内容制作、分享、投稿、发行音乐或声音作品" - making and
  releasing music from it, commercial or not.
- Published work must say, visibly, that it is AI-synthesised ("需在明显位置注明相关内容使用 AI
  （人工智能）技术合成") and name "绮萱" and "DiffSinger".
- Programs like Cantoria: "编写与开发新的推理代码和程序，以支持模型所包含的功能" - writing new
  inference code and programs for the model's functions is allowed. Forbidden: reverse-engineering,
  converting, modifying, fine-tuning, splitting or merging the weights, distilling.
- Dictionaries, phoneme lists and configs may be changed or extended to fit other callers.
- Redistribution: allowed, but the whole package, the terms, weights and picture unchanged; not
  sold; the receiver told it is free and to read the terms before use. Within the PRC, the deep
  synthesis rules apply.
- Its output may not train voice synthesis or conversion. The character picture may not be used
  commercially or in AI-image work.

## OpenVPI vocoders (openvpi.github.io/vocoders)

"The model weights are licensed under the CC BY-NC-SA 4.0"; "Redistribution and integration
into applications are permitted under the terms of this license"; "Synthesizing or producing
commercial work with this vocoder is not constrained by the license". Whoever distributes it
includes the licence and credits OpenVPI / the DiffSinger community.

## What it means for F

The best fit is **Qixuan with OpenVPI's pc_nsf_hifigan 2025.02** (about 450 MB, two GitHub
release files): the only DiffSinger voice found whose terms allow both redistribution and songs
for money, and which name a program like Cantoria in so many words. Open questions before
offering it: how it sings Japanese and English (Mandarin is its own; the others are by language
prefix), whether Cantoria reads its package and the pc_nsf_hifigan vocoder as they are, and what
the offer shows (the terms, the AI notice, the credit).

The cleanest licence is VOICEVOX's (the voice models may be built into an application), but its
engine is a 1.8 GB download; a lighter way would be voicevox_core + `s0.vvm` + its ONNX Runtime,
if core's song functions can be called. Sinsy is a small fully free fallback, but needs a new
backend and sings far below the others.

Not checked: whether Nishiren bundles its vocoder; whether voicevox_core's downloader shows the
terms; the terms of VOICEVOX's ONNX Runtime; the full COPYING of the Sinsy voice.
